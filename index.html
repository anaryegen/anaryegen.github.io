Anar Yeginbergen

PhD Candidate in Natural Language Processing

I am a Ph.D candidate at HiTZ: Basque Center for Language Technology, University of the Basque Country, supervised by Rodrigo Agerri.

Factuality Retrieval Augmented Generation Information Extraction Multilingual NLP Low-resource NLP Argument Mining

Prior to my PhD, I was awarded the Erasmus Mundus European Masters Program in Language and Communication Technologies (LCT) scholarship, completing a double M.Sc in Natural Language Processing at the University of Lorraine (France) and the University of the Basque Country (Spain). I also hold a BSc in Computer Science, with early research experience in question-answering systems and discourse parsing.
