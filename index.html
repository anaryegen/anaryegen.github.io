HiTZ Center, University of the Basque Country

Anar Yeginbergen

I am a Ph.D candidate at HiTZ: Basque Center for Language Technology, the University of the Basque Country where I am supervised by Rodrigo Agerri.
I work on factuality, information extraction, multilingual and low-resource NLP.

Prior to the Ph.D, I was awarded The Erasmus Mundus European Masters Program in Language and Communication Technologies (LCT) scholarship for a double M.Sc in Natural Language Processing in 2022 at the Univerity of Lorraine (France) and the University of the Basque Country (Spain).

Additionally, I hold BSc in Computer Science. My journey in NLP began with my BSc thesis on Question-answering systems and then the research on Discourse Parsing and Argument Mining during two years of the MSc degree.
